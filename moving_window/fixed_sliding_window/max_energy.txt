## Problem 1: Maximum Energy Output
Difficulty: Easy
Pattern: Fixed Sliding Window
Problem Statement:
You are monitoring a grid of solar panels arranged in a straight line. You are given an array of integers energy,
where energy[i] represents the power generated by the i-th solar panel. You are also given an integer k.
A power inverter can only process energy from exactly k consecutive solar panels at a time.
Return the maximum total energy that can be captured from any k consecutive solar panels.
Example 1:

* Input: energy = [1, 8, 3, 0, 5, 10, 2], k = 3
* Output: 17
* Explanation: The window of size 3 with the maximum sum is [5, 10, 2], which sums to 5 + 10 + 2 = 17.

Example 2:

* Input: energy = [4, 2, 1, 7, 8, 1, 2, 8, 1, 0], k = 4
* Output: 19
* Explanation: The window [8, 1, 2, 8] gives the maximum sum of 8 + 1 + 2 + 8 = 19.

Constraints:

* 1 ≤ k ≤ energy.length ≤ 10⁵
* $-10^4 \le \text{energy}[i] \le 10^4$
