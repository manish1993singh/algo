**Problem Statement: Merge Overlapping Intervals**

Given an array of time intervals where $\text{intervals}[i] = [\text{start}_i, \text{end}_i]$, merge all overlapping intervals and return an array of non-overlapping intervals that cover all the intervals in the input.

---

**Function Description**

Complete the `mergeIntervals` function.

`mergeIntervals` has the following parameter(s):

* `int intervals[n][2]`: a 2D array of integers representing the start and end times of $n$ intervals.

**Returns**

* `int[][2]`: a 2D array of non-overlapping intervals sorted by their start time.

---

**Input Format**

* The first line contains an integer, $n$, the number of intervals.
* The next $n$ lines each contain two space-separated integers, $\text{start}_i$ and $\text{end}_i$, denoting the start and end of the $i$-th interval.

---

**Constraints**

* $1 \le n \le 10^5$
* $0 \le \text{start}_i \le \text{end}_i \le 10^9$

---

**Sample Input 0**

```text
4
1 3
2 6
8 10
15 18

```

**Sample Output 0**

```text
1 6
8 10
15 18

```

**Explanation 0**

Intervals `[1, 3]` and `[2, 6]` overlap, so they are merged into `[1, 6]`. The remaining intervals do not overlap.

---

**Sample Input 1**

```text
2
1 4
4 5

```

**Sample Output 1**

```text
1 5

```

**Explanation 1**

Intervals `[1, 4]` and `[4, 5]` share a boundary point at `4`, so they are considered overlapping and merged into `[1, 5]`.
