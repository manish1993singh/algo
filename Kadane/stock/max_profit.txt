## Problem 6: Maximum Stock Profit Streak
Difficulty: Medium
Pattern 6: Kadane's Algorithm
Problem Statement:
You are tracking the daily price fluctuations of a stock. You are given an integer array changes, where changes[i] represents the net change in the stock price on the i-th day (positive means the price went up, negative means it went down).
Find the maximum cumulative profit you could achieve by holding the stock during any contiguous sequence of days. If every day results in a net loss, your maximum profit is 0 (by choosing not to hold the stock at all).
Example 1:

* Input: changes = [-2, 1, -3, 4, -1, 2, 1, -5, 4]
* Output: 6
* Explanation: The contiguous day sequence [4, -1, 2, 1] has the largest cumulative sum of 6.

Example 2:

* Input: changes = [1]
* Output: 1

Example 3:

* Input: changes = [-5, -2, -3]
* Output: 0
* Explanation: Since all changes are negative, the best strategy is to avoid holding the stock, yielding a profit of 0.

Constraints:

* 1 <= changes.length <= 10⁵
* -10⁴ <= changes[i] <= 10⁴
